# Learnings

What went wrong in past QA runs and the rule that prevents it. Read this before a run. Add to it
after a major failure, as [docs/qa-pr-instructions.md](docs/qa-pr-instructions.md#learnings)
describes.

## Android

- android expression-details sheet drags and long cases (2026-11-10, [QA of speak-android#9191](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/9191)): about 40 minutes lost. Causes: a drag that started at 40% of the screen began on the scrim of the Navigation 3 sheet (half height, 50%) and closed it, while the controller sheet (peek, about 70%) took the same drag; the immersive AI lesson setup takes 4 to 7 minutes in Maestro, so a recording started before it hit the 180 s `screenrecord` limit before the case's arrival; and editing a case script while bash still ran it broke the run. Rules: start sheet drags below the middle of the screen, and expect a half height Navigation 3 sheet to close on a drag down (`expression-details/drag-at-top.yaml` and `back-to-origin.yaml` already allow it); start the recording after slow setup flows (`ask-ai-lesson-tutor.yaml` is split from the tap for that); copy the case script before editing it mid-run.
- android singles carousel card tap (2026-11-10, [QA of speak-android#9178](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/9178)): case 8 lost about 15 minutes. Cause: `tapOn` with `childOf: id: singles_carousel_item` resolves the parent to the first matching carousel item only, so any card after the first was "Element not found" although `maestro hierarchy` showed it. Rule: pick a repeated item by its own text and id, without `childOf` on the repeated parent (applied in `android/flows/singles/details-open-card.yaml`).
- android phone-speak (2026-11-09, [QA of speak-android#9133](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/9133)): two base passes lost about 15 minutes. Causes: the call timer reads `01:05` (two-digit minutes), so a `^1:..` wait never matched and calls ran past the 180 s `screenrecord` limit; and the survey sheet is its own window, so a `dump` taken once it shows sees only the sheet, not "Preparing feedback..." under it. Rule: wait for `text:^01:(0[2-9]|[1-5][0-9])$` to hold a call over 60 s, start `rec` after the opener flow, and `dump` once right after End call, before the survey arrives.

- The automotive (AAOS emulator) cases for Free Talk immersive lost about 40 minutes to setup ([QA of speak-android#8992](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/8992), 2026-11-09). Causes: `qa_android.py login` starts `com.selabs.speak.SpeakActivity`, which is not exported on `automotiveDevelopDebug` (`SecurityException`); `speak-dev://debug-menu` resolves to no activity there; `qa_android.py shot` writes a "Multiple displays" warning ahead of the PNG because AAOS has a cluster display; the app runs as user 10, so `pm grant` without `--user 10` leaves the mic prompt over the lesson; and the pre-lesson start button sits below the fold in landscape. Rule: sign in with `qa_android.py bridge login`, open the app and Debug Menu with `-e AUTOMOTIVE=true` (`common/launch-home-automotive.yaml`, `common/open-debug-menu-automotive.yaml`), strip the bytes before `\x89PNG` in AAOS shots, grant with `pm grant --user 10`, and let the flow `scrollUntilVisible` to `ai_lesson_pre_lesson_start`. `adb screenrecord` on AAOS emits no frames while the screen is still, so a static case mp4 can be only a few seconds long.
- Phone speak ring microphone case lost about 20 minutes ([QA of speak-android#8712](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/8712), 2026-11-09). Causes: a failed earlier case left Ring my phone on (the server keeps it), so the next case's tap turned the ring off and no microphone prompt could show; and once the app has asked for the microphone, reinstalling with `install -r` keeps its asked-before state, so a later ring on shows only the notice. Rule: each ring flow ends with the ring off, so after any failed ring case reset with `pm clear` and a fresh login, check the ring is off before the next case, and revoke the mic with `pm revoke` plus `pm clear-permission-flags <pkg> android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO user-set user-fixed`.
- android storybook process death (2026-11-08, [QA of speak-android#9107](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/9107)): Developer options > Background process limit > No background processes did not kill the dev app on the API 34 AVD; `pidof` showed the same pid 15 s after Home on all four runs, so the first pass proved nothing about process death. Rule: set the limit with `system-settings/set-background-process-limit.yaml` when asked, but still record `pidof` before and after Home, and fall back to `am kill` once a second until `pidof` is empty. Log which one killed it.
- Don't keep activities cases on the API 34 AVD lost about 15 minutes ([speak-android#9084](https://github.com/usespeakeasy/speak-android/pull/9084), 2026-11-08). Cause: `adb shell settings put global always_finish_activities 1` only writes the setting; the activity manager keeps activities alive, so Home and relaunch showed no `view destroyed` / `instance state restored` lines and the case proved nothing. `settings put global development_settings_enabled 1` alone also leaves the Developer options intent on `DevelopmentSettingsDisabledActivity`. Rule: turn on Developer options with `android/flows/system-settings/enable-developer-options.yaml`, flip the switch with `toggle-dont-keep-activities.yaml`, check `settings get global always_finish_activities`, and confirm `MainController view destroyed` in the lifecycle log before trusting the case. Flip it outside the case recording; the Settings scroll takes about 90 s.
- The Android 4.74.0 global regression lost about 40 minutes to two causes. A flow env default with a `$` in it (`LOOP_TEXT: ${LOOP_TEXT || "^Introduction$"}`) was not evaluated, so `tapOn` looked for the literal `${LOOP_TEXT || ...}` text and the 4.73.0 lesson cases failed three times; and an `adb logcat` started by an earlier run was never stopped, so the base build's lifecycle file kept collecting the branch run's lines and a per-case diff compared the branch with itself (0 lines of diff). - Video lesson video-controls, buffering and case 1 completion checks failed for about 60 minutes on both builds. Causes: after a segment's last card the "Tap any word" tooltip pauses playback (`pausePlaybackForTooltip`) until a word is tapped, so the video never resumed; the player controls auto-hide about 2 s after a tap, faster than a Maestro `tapOn` plus `assertVisible` or a harness `tap` (which waits for a fresh tree); and `dump`/`has`/`wait` hang or return nothing while the video plays because the tree never goes idle. ## iOS

- speak-ios#9918 QA (2026-11-08) was QA incomplete: the iPhone Duo cases could not run. The macOS VM's Xcode (iOS 27.0 sims) lists no iPhone Duo device type. After installing the iOS 27.1 runtime, its profile names `com.apple.CoreSimulator.SimDeviceType.V68`, but `simctl create` rejects that as `Invalid device type`. Rule: when a PR's cases need the iPhone Duo (folded or unfolded), check `xcrun simctl list devicetypes` for it before building. If it's missing, say in the first reply that the Duo cases are unreachable on the VM.
- Native chat AI lessons on the macOS VM simulator failed before the first turn ("this lesson is unable to play", then `Initialize: RPC timeout. Apparently deadlocked. Aborting now.`) on both builds; restarting coreaudiod, rebooting the sim and a mic-state bypass did not help. Rule: when a LiveKit lesson must run on the VM, apply the same uncommitted QA-only patch to both trees in `AudioIOController.init`: `#if targetEnvironment(simulator) try? AudioManager.shared.setManualRenderingMode(true) #endif` (plus a `return` at the top of `updateMicrophoneState`), and put its diff in the toggle. With it the lesson, Tutor Everywhere and its tutor reply ran.
- Maestro 2.12.1 `takeScreenshot` with an absolute path (`${SHOT}` = `/Users/...`) fails with no reason in the log. Rule: pass a relative name and run `maestro test --test-output-dir <dir>`; the PNGs land under `<dir>/**/takeScreenshot/` at full resolution (1206x2622).
